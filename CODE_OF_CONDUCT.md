# Code of Conduct

This repository is a shared learning environment for members of the KFUPM Computer Club. The following expectations apply to all participation: issues, pull requests, reviews, and discussions.

## Expectations

- Treat every contributor with respect, regardless of experience level.
- Review the work, not the person. Feedback must be specific, factual, and actionable.
- Ask for help when blocked, and offer help when asked.
- Credit the work of others. Do not remove or alter attribution.
- Do not publish personal information about any person without their consent.
- Keep discussions on topic and professional.

## Reporting

Concerns about conduct should be raised privately with a maintainer: Adib Alqurashi ([@adib-alqurashi](https://github.com/adib-alqurashi)) or Yogesh Krishna Thakur ([@YKrishna17](https://github.com/YKrishna17)). Reports are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content that violates these expectations and may restrict a contributor's access to the repository. The club's own regulations apply in addition to this document.
