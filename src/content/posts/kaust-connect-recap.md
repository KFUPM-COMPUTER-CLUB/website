On 12 October we opened the doors at 7 PM for KAUST Connect. KAUST Academy alumni from every track came to tell us how they got in, what the programs are like from the inside, and what they would do differently.

## Who came

More than 400 students showed up, and not only from KFUPM. Students from Imam Abdulrahman Bin Faisal University and King Faisal University made the trip, which turned the Q&A into a conversation between campuses rather than a lecture.

## What stood out

- Every speaker had a different path in. There is no single "right" profile for the academy.
- The questions that got the longest answers were about timing: when to apply, and what to build before applying.
- Coffee came from our sponsor OKO, and it did not last long.

## Thank you

To the speakers, to the Deanship of Student Affairs for the support, and to everyone who stayed after the session to keep asking questions. The photos are on our X account, and the next talk will be announced there first.
