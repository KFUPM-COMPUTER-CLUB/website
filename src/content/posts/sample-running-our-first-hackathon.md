Last spring we ran our first full 24-hour hackathon. Here is the short version of what went right and what went wrong.

## Logistics beat ideas

We spent weeks on the theme and two days on logistics. That ratio should have been reversed. Power strips, Wi-Fi capacity, and a quiet room for sleeping mattered more than the theme ever did.

## Publish the rubric early

Teams kept asking what the judges cared about. Next time the rubric goes on the registration page.

## Mentors are the product

Every team that shipped had a mentor stop by in the first two hours. Recruit mentors before you recruit participants.
