We rebuilt the club website with one goal: make it easy for any member to publish.

## What changed

- **No database and no logins.** Every page is a plain file. Events, articles, and newsletter issues live in a small content folder.
- **Members write.** Send a draft through the Write for us page and it goes up with your name on it.
- **Every button goes somewhere real.** Register, subscribe, join, follow.

## What is next

A monthly newsletter, a public events calendar, and short write-ups from every workshop we run.
