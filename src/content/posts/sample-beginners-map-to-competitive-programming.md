Competitive programming has a brutal start. This is the path that worked for most people in our ladder.

## Weeks 1 to 2: syntax and arrays

Solve 800-rated problems on Codeforces until they feel boring. That usually takes 30 to 40 problems.

## Weeks 3 to 6: the toolbox

Prefix sums, two pointers, binary search, basic greedy. One topic a week, ten problems per topic.

## Week 7 onward: contests

Join every Div. 3 and Div. 4 round. Upsolve one problem after each contest. Rating comes later; consistency comes first.
