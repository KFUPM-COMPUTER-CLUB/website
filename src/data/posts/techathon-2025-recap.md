Techathon brought teams from universities across the Kingdom to Building 22. More than 60 teams applied, 24 reached the on-site final, and the prize pool passed 30,000 SAR.

## Day one: design

The first day started with a three-hour design phase. Teams had to turn an idea into a scoped plan before writing code, and the mentors spent most of those hours pushing back on scope.

## The final

On the last day 11 teams presented to the jury, with Dr. Khalid Al-Anzi and Dr. Abdullah Al-Sultan in attendance. The judging rewarded working demos and clear thinking, not slides.

## Winners

- First place: Team Belligol, KFUPM
- Second place: Team Muarshf, Jubail Industrial College
- Third place: Team Mifaq, KFUPM
- Best design: Team CodeSparksYU, Al Yamamah University
- Best idea: Team Fussha, Jubail Industrial College

## What we learned

Judging criteria should be public from the first announcement. Power strips run out before pizza does. And four days is long enough to build something real, as long as day one is spent on the plan.

Thank you to the Techathon team for the sleepless nights, and to every team that came. See you next spring.
